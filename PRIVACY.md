# Real Grade privacy policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Real Grade runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and no account, and it
does not send anything about you anywhere.

**What it reads.** On airbnb.com pages you open, it reads what the page already shows:
listing ratings, review counts, prices, the reviews loaded on the page, and the map
location of the listing or search.

**What it stores.** Your last 20 searches (for up to 30 days) are kept in your
browser's extension storage, so a listing can be compared with the other places in
your search: listing IDs, ratings, review counts, bedrooms and prices. It also stores
whether you minimized the card. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

**What it sends.** Nothing to us or any third party. The only network requests it
makes are to airbnb.com, the site you are already on, to re-read a listing page after
you move between listings in the app, or to read a search page's location.

**What it never does.** No analytics, tracking, ads, selling or sharing of data. It
never reads your messages, payment details or account information.
